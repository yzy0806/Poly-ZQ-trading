# Execution safety and recovery

For workstation paths and setup, see [macOS development](local-development-macos.md).
For dated deployment status, see the [production record](../deploy/CURRENT_DEPLOYMENT_AND_SECURITY.md).

1. **Scope.** This implementation unifies order identity, fill accounting, and reconciliation; adds safe halt and bounded shutdown; and isolates simulated and real execution data. It does not change the heartbeat implementation or IBKR account-summary filtering. The strategy remains BUY-only: no automatic flattening, selling, redemption, or position repair is added.

2. **Durable order identity.** A real hedge's signed payload and exchange order hash are committed before POST. Timeouts, lost replies, crashes, 404 lookups, and missing open-order entries cannot release its reservation. Recovery queries the original hash and may retransmit the identical signed payload after a delay. It never signs a different replacement while the earlier outcome is unresolved. A cancellation acknowledgement alone is insufficient: terminal venue status, cumulative matched quantity, and associated trade IDs must agree with the ledger before any remaining quantity can be replaced.

3. **Fill accounting.** Each trade/order pair retains its entire actual quantity and price. Confirmed fills are allocated to the obligation only up to the amount owed; the remainder stays visible as excess exposure and remains in portfolio cost and quantity. MATCHED, MINED, and RETRYING quantities reserve shares while settlement is pending. CONFIRMED credits the obligation; FAILED releases settlement credit only while preserving the raw record and separate order reservation. Contradictory terminal settlement updates remain reserved for manual investigation. Unidentified venue fills remain in the durable event journal and block reconciliation. Trade semantics follow the [Polymarket order lifecycle](https://docs.polymarket.com/concepts/order-lifecycle).

4. **Reconciliation.** All durable batches and obligations participate, including older completed batches whose fills later require attention. The comparison includes IBKR open/completed orders, executions and target-contract inventory, plus Polymarket open orders, full paginated trade history and all configured event-token positions. Unknown orders are not automatically cancelled on the other account/client's behalf. They block new entries and trigger cancellation of this strategy's working ZQ. Missing completion evidence, pending settlement, stale snapshots, or interrupted reads cannot produce CLEAN. `RECONCILIATION_MAX_AGE_SECONDS` is required; the example environment sets it to 60 seconds. A saved clean result cannot authorize entry after another execution-ledger mutation.

    Foreign-client orders explicitly identified as ZQ futures in a different contract month are outside this strategy's order comparison. An October strategy therefore permits an independently managed September ZQ order. Identification requires a valid contract month, security type and positive permanent order ID; a permanent ID matching a strategy ledger order is never excluded. Status callbacks have no contract details, so they inherit scope only from the exact client/order/permanent-ID combination. During a fresh open-order read, an unidentified status blocks entry until contract details or the end marker arrive; anything still unidentified then retains the safety pause. Disconnect discards remembered scope. Current-month and unidentified orders remain blocking, and account-wide margin and excess-liquidity checks still include all account exposure.

5. **Restart.** The process starts disarmed. Before real hedge submission resumes, fresh venue evidence must rule out unexplained inventory, excess, unknown orders and unidentified fills. Known outstanding obligations may then be hedged while new ZQ entries remain blocked. This prevents blindly buying another hedge when an operator placed one while the engine was offline. IBKR open-order absence is not cancellation evidence; recovery requires a matching completion/status callback. Historical data outside the venue's available execution/completion window may require operator reconstruction.

6. **Halt and shutdown.** Emergency halt immediately prevents new ZQ entry and requests cancellation of every working strategy ZQ order, independent of residual profitability or slow hedge requests. It keeps processing late fills and permits the existing capped hedge mandate. Application shutdown does the same while keeping callbacks and hedge workers alive for up to `SHUTDOWN_DRAIN_SECONDS` (required; example value 20). `ENGINE_STOP` records whether reconciliation completed. A deadline, disconnect, or forced termination cannot guarantee cancellation at the venue; unresolved state must be recovered after restart. Keep the drain deadline below the container's 30-second grace period. Network execution operations use `EXECUTION_REQUEST_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` (required; example value 10).

7. **Database transition.** Back up the existing database before adopting this release. The additive schema stores the execution environment and raw venue receipts. A fresh database binds to IBKR mode, account and API client ID, plus Polymarket simulation mode, wallet, configured API owner, host and chain. The October release also binds the ZQ contract month, Polymarket event ID and rate-effective date; an earlier identity cannot be silently relabelled, even for the same account. Identities use fingerprints rather than credential values. Live execution refuses simulated or unsigned records. Switching between simulation and real trading, paper and live IBKR, account/wallet identities, or strategy events/calendars requires a separate database. Credential-owner changes also require deliberate reconciliation rather than automatic relabelling.

8. **Legacy data.** A pre-change database with execution history has no trustworthy provenance and may already contain clipped fills. Execution startup therefore refuses to bless it automatically; `READ_ONLY` remains available for inspection. Preserve the original and reconstruct actual fills, costs, orders and obligations from venue evidence before migration. There is no automatic migration or reconciliation override for this case. For a flat starting point, verify no outstanding strategy orders or inventory at either venue, retain the old database as history, and configure a fresh live ledger. Never empty a database to conceal an unresolved position.

9. **VPS files and backups.** Use distinct paths, for example `sqlite+aiosqlite:////var/lib/zq-arb/paper.sqlite3` and `sqlite+aiosqlite:////var/lib/zq-arb/live.sqlite3`. The default `engine.sqlite3` name is also supported and is bound by the same identity rules. Keep databases directly in `/var/lib/zq-arb`, with one engine writer per ledger. Deploy the updated backup script so each `*.sqlite3` ledger is backed up separately. An older executable does not enforce these invariants even though it can open the additive schema; any rollback involving execution should remain READ_ONLY until reviewed.

10. **Release evidence.** Offline regression tests cover uncertain POST outcomes and identical replay, concurrent routing, cancellation/fill races, raw excess cost, trade lifecycle conflicts and duplicates, unidentified/multiple maker fills, inventory/order discrepancies, stale reads, restart gating, late fills during halt/shutdown, and database identity mismatches. SDK hash fixtures cover EOA and deposit-wallet signature types and acknowledgement checks. These checks do not establish actual broker/CLOB connectivity, VPS behavior, or resolution of the separately reviewed heartbeat issue.

11. **IBKR callback convergence.** A previously reconciled session can briefly receive position, execution, and order-status callbacks out of order. A gap explainable by a known strategy BUY order enters UNKNOWN and blocks new entries without automatically disarming the operator's existing mandate. Actual executions alone create hedge obligations. `IBKR_CALLBACK_SETTLE_SECONDS` is required, with an example value of 2 seconds; add it to existing local/VPS environment files before starting this release. It has no config fallback and does not control Polymarket settlement time. An independent watchdog escalates an unresolved gap even when no further callbacks arrive. Duplicate callbacks and account refreshes cannot extend that deadline. A fresh completed read that still disagrees, or an independent unexplained order, execution, identity, inventory difference, or excess hedge, invokes the safety pause and residual cancellation response.

12. **IBKR refresh and order status.** A gap requests an immediate read through the same single-flight coordinator used by periodic account refreshes. Execution requests have unique IDs; an old execution-end marker cannot complete a newer request. Streams without request IDs are serialized. A timed-out refresh is not reusable: late completion markers cannot restore CLEAN, and the runtime reconnects before another read. Terminal order callbacks remove observed-open membership. A delayed working callback for a terminal order requires fresh validation rather than reopening the durable order. Cached clean state is invalidated by venue revisions as well as ledger revisions. Unknown venue receipts block hedge submission even when an account read is incomplete; recovery must identify existing orders rather than create exposure to explain an unknown fill.

    Fill-gap convergence is independent of snapshot completion. Once a session has reconciled cleanly, the coordinator retains its accepted positions and updates them from subsequent position callbacks while the separate account snapshot rebuilds. It compares that evidence with durable executions, cumulative reported fills, terminal completion, and order conflicts on every reconciliation attempt. Matching evidence clears the callback gap and cancels its watchdog without waiting for snapshot end markers. A completed position read replaces the retained view, including omitted positions; disconnect discards it, and startup cannot use it before a clean baseline. The full refresh and Polymarket reconciliation must still finish before new entry is authorized, and the independent account-refresh timeout remains enforced. Resolution audits include the quantities that agreed and whether the account refresh was still pending. The callback setting remains 2 seconds in the example environment.

13. **Pause explanations and activation.** The dashboard shows pending reconciliation separately from a retained safety or operator pause cause and timestamp. Audit actions record callback-gap start, resolution, expiry, and safety pauses. CLEAN does not automatically clear a manual pause, emergency halt, expired callback deadline, or confirmed mismatch. Restart loads the new code but starts disarmed and requires fresh venue reconciliation. This update neither purges the ledger nor synthesizes opening inventory or executions.

14. **Scheduled Gateway maintenance.** `IBKR_ACCOUNT_REFRESH_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` is a separate required setting, set to 30 seconds in the example environment. The execution request and callback convergence deadlines remain 10 and 2 seconds. With maintenance enabled, the engine blocks entries and cancels working ZQ one minute before the regular 16:00–17:00 America/Chicago break. Confirmed cancellations, completed hedges and fresh clean reconciliation certify the drain. Only a drained cycle with a connectivity interruption beginning near the configured 16:10 Gateway restart can reconnect without latching an account-refresh timeout pause. Timed-out reads remain invalid; independent safety faults still pause. The bounded exception ends on recovery or at 17:05. Fresh reconciliation and a restored connection release the hold at reopening without changing arming permission. Manual disarm, pause and halt remain effective. Friday's entry hold continues through Sunday 17:00; special holiday schedules are not implemented. See [maintenance validation and rollout](validation/ibkr-maintenance-2026-09-21.md) for configuration and checks.
